Why the mark drops tiles
A launcher icon is often drawn at 48–72px. Seven tiles across 1024px means each letter has ~140px of cap height; at 48px that's 6px — below the point where Baloo's soft terminals survive. So Tier 2 keeps only W and H, size-stepped so H dominates, and Tier 3 keeps the amber H alone. Every tier is still a bevelled tile with the same radius ratio, so they read as one family.
Export notes
Opaque, zero alpha, square corners: icon.png, icon-dark.png, icon-tinted.png, favicon.png. Alpha required: adaptive-icon.png, adaptive-icon-monochrome.png, splash-icon.png, splash-icon-dark.png, notification-icon.png. Never pre-round an iOS icon — the OS masks it, and a baked corner leaves a dark halo. Export at 1× the stated size; Expo downscales better from one clean 1024 than from hand-made small versions.
