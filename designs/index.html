Reading the files
Screens are laid out left to right in flow order, light mode above and dark mode below, so any screen can be compared vertically. Each device frame has a caption underneath with its number, name, route, and the state it shows. Some files are wide — scroll horizontally, or zoom out with ⌘− / Ctrl−.
